Gingerbread House Builder

Build your own gingerbread house

Pick a building, tweak the dimensions and decorate it with icing. Every part comes out of the oven as STEP, STL, DXF and SVG, with tabs that slot together.

Pick a building and cut it from sheet material. The icing becomes an engraving, and you get SVG and DXF files ready for LightBurn, with tabs that slot together.

Parts

One .zip per part with STEP, STL, DXF and SVG One .zip per part with LightBurn SVG and DXF, plus STEP
In LightBurn: open laser_sheet.svg (or the DXF) from the zip. Red lines import as a cut layer, black shapes as an engrave layer: set the red layer to Line (cut) and the black layer to Fill (engrave), and set your kerf offset on the cut layer.
Material: all parts are laid out on a bed of the width you set, for the sheet thickness you set. The tabs and slots are sized for that thickness, so measure your sheet. The chimney is left out because it is a solid block.
In Fusion: Insert → Insert DXF and choose one sketch per layer. Extrude layer PLATE by the plate thickness and layer ICING by the icing height on top. The STEP files are ready-made solids with plate and icing as separate bodies.
Printing: print_plate.stl in the zip lays everything flat on your print bed. Add a filament change at 1.8 mm for white icing on brown gingerbread.
